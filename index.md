## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to MPTray! This is a simple music player for Windows 10 and 11 that sits in your system tray. With MPTray, you can easily control your music without disrupting your workflow. Let’s get started on how to download and run MPTray!

## 📥 Download & Install

1. Visit the [MPTray Releases page](https://github.com/emanriquezs/MPTray/releases).

2. Look for the latest version. The version will typically be the one at the top of the list.

3. Download the setup file. The file will have a name like `MPTraySetup.exe`. Click on it to start the download.

4. Once the download completes, locate the file in your Downloads folder (or wherever your downloads are saved).

5. Double-click the setup file (MPTraySetup.exe) to start the installation.

6. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

7. Once installation is complete, you can find MPTray in your Start Menu or on your desktop. 

8. Double-click the MPTray icon to launch the application.

## ⚙️ System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11
- **.NET Framework:** Ensure you have the latest version of .NET installed. MPTray uses .NET technologies to function smoothly.
- **Storage:** About 100 MB available storage required for installation.
- **Memory:** A minimum of 4 GB of RAM is recommended for optimal performance.

## 🎵 Features

- **Tray Integration:** MPTray runs silently in your system tray, allowing you to control your music without interrupting your other tasks.
- **Easy Access:** Quickly play, pause, skip songs, and adjust volume right from the tray icon.
- **Media Library Support:** Easily manage and play your favorite music files.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Enjoy a clean and simple interface designed for easy navigation and control.
- **Mica Effects:** Experience modern UI design with Mica effects for a pleasing visual experience on Windows.

## 🎧 How to Use MPTray

1. **Open MPTray**: After installing MPTray, locate the icon in your system tray at the bottom right of your screen.

2. **Add Music**: Click on the MPTray icon and select the option to add your favorite music files or folders.

3. **Control Playback**: Use the tray menu to play, pause, or skip tracks. You can also adjust the volume directly from the tray.

4. **Settings**: Right-click the MPTray icon to access settings where you can customize your experience.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **App Won't Launch:** Make sure your operating system is updated. If problems persist, try reinstalling the application.

- **Audio Issues:** Ensure your speakers are connected and your system volume is adjust correctly.

- **Playlist Not Found:** Check the file location of your music library and ensure files are accessible.
